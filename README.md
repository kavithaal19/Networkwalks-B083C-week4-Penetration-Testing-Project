## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a 5-day black-box penetration testing assessment of Mediroza General Hospital’s website. The objective is to identify security vulnerabilities, demonstrate their impact by accessing confidential patient reports and other sensitive hospital information, and document the findings with appropriate risk ratings and remediation recommendations.     WK4-Mediroza

---


## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Identify vulnerabilities in the Mediroza Hospital web application.
- Gain authorised access to restricted areas of the website.
- Retrieve three confidential patient laboratory reports as proof of impact.
- Assess and recover the protected/encrypted PDF contents.
- Identify further sensitive data exposure, including employee salaries and shareholder details.
- Document all findings and evidence with appropriate risk ratings.
- Provide remediation recommendations to help the hospital improve its security.
